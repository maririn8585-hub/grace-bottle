## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: finished square mobile home-screen icon for GRACE BOTTLE, a Japanese bar's customer bottle-keep inventory app. Create one elegant, highly legible icon: a large dark amber whisky bottle with gold cap and ivory label, with a small inventory tag bearing a bold checkmark attached to its neck. Bottle silhouette instantly recognizable at 60px. Warm solid ivory background #faf6ec filling entire square edge to edge; palette dark espresso #332c20, warm gold #c3986a and muted gold #9c7a42. Refined slightly dimensional illustration, crisp broad shapes, very subtle glass highlight, minimal detail. Entire bottle and tag centered inside middle 70% of square, comfortable safe margins for home-screen cropping. No text, letters, numbers, glasses, extra bottles, border, rounded-square framing, phone mockup, watermark or transparency. Square 1024x1024 production icon.
